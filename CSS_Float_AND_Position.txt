
[*] Positioning Property:

[1]Static: Default. Element stays in the normal document flow.

[2] Relative: Element is positioned relative to its normal position using top/right/bottom/left.

[3] Absolute: Positioned relative to the nearest positioned ancestor. Removed from normal flow.

[4] Fixed: Positioned relative to the viewport. Stays fixed during scroll.

[5] Sticky: A hybrid of relative and fixed. Sticks at a defined position during scrolling.


[*] Float Property:

(1) Float: left: Element floats to the left.

(2) Float: right: Element floats to the right.

(3) Float: none: Default. Element does not float.
